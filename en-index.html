FAQ

GOOD TO KNOW.

Can I preorder directly from the site?

Yes. You can choose a format, set the quantity and send your preorder by email. Italprotein will follow up to confirm availability, shipping and payment.

What are the prices?

The 1 kg jar costs €50 with shipping included. The 200 g resealable pouch costs €10 with shipping excluded. The box of 100 sachets of 5 g costs €25.

When will the product arrive?

All formats are currently sold as preorders and the first batches are still being finalized. For that reason, arrival is estimated in about 1 month after ordering.

Are the 5 g sachets sold individually?

No. The €25 price refers to a box of 100 sachets of 5 g, designed primarily for bars, cafés and Ho.Re.Ca. use.

How do I choose the right format?

If you want the best quantity-to-price ratio, the 1 kg jar is the best-value option. If you want a simple entry point, the 200 g pouch is the easiest way to start. If you work in coffee service, the sachet box is the most suitable format.
