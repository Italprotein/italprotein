FAQ

GOOD TO KNOW.

What is Proamina®?

It is a food-grade amino-acid blend consisting primarily of glycine and L-serine, developed by NH2 Tech and distributed by Italprotein.

Can I pay online today?

You can already send a preorder enquiry by email. Online checkout is not active yet, and Italprotein will manually confirm pricing, availability and payment terms.

How much Proamina® should I use?

Dosage depends on the application, pH and formulation goals. Start with small trials and request technical guidance for professional applications.

Are all three formats in stock?

They are currently offered as preorders while the project is being finalized. Estimated arrival is about 1 month from the order date, subject to confirmation by Italprotein.

Is a 5 g sachet the recommended dose?

No. 5 g refers to the pack content, not a universal recommended dosage. Usage depends on the food formulation and application.
